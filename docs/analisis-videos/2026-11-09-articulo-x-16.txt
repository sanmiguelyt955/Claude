1. the 3D "what if" backyard
a 60 to 75 second 3D Short. a house sits over a soil block cut open like a cake while a calm narrator builds an everyday thing, one problem at a time.
You Built It's 48 TikToks have 58.6M views since oct 11. its YouTube Shorts, sorted by views on oct 30:
its second-biggest Short, a strip of dirt that becomes a road, has 5,257,944 views:
how it's made: the page doesn't say. on screen it's semi-real 3D, a soil block cut open like a cake and one caption word at a time over a calm, fast narrator.
our guide rebuilds it as one paste. Claude Code writes the script, voices it on your PC, builds the yard in Blender (free) and adds the captions.
its worked example is this pond, so running it gives you this exact video (yes, the fish too):
a blind rater gave it 59/70, while You Built It's own videos scored 55 and 61 on it (it's ACTUALLY harsh). the weak row is the look, since our fish are simpler than their yard.
to start:
-> save whatif_guide.md as CLAUDE.md in an empty folder
-> open Claude Code there and say "make one video, you pick the topic"
it takes about 2.5 to 3 hours on a gaming PC, mostly unattended. the format is You Built It's, this is an independent rebuild.
the guide: https://github.com/Theoxmotion/whatif-3d-studio
