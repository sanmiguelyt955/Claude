# Textos para tickets de soporte de GitHub (Actions bloqueadas)

Dónde: https://support.github.com/contact → **Account / Billing** o **GitHub Actions**. Envía en inglés (responden más rápido).
Antes de enviar, cambia lo que está entre `[corchetes]`. No pongas contraseñas ni tokens.

**Asunto:** GitHub Actions disabled on my account — previous tickets answered but never resolved

>
> I have contacted Support before about this ([ticket #NÚMERO, fecha] / [ticket #NÚMERO, fecha]). I received replies, but the issue was never followed up or resolved, and the tickets were closed while Actions remained blocked.
>
> What I need:
> 1. The specific reason Actions is disabled on my account (billing, verification, policy flag, etc.).
> 2. What I must do to restore it, or confirmation that you have restored it.
> 3. Please keep this ticket open until Actions works again; I can test immediately and confirm.
>
> Details: personal account, [Free / Pro] plan, country: [México], I use Actions only for my own repositories (deployment/automation scripts). I have not received any email about a suspension or policy violation [cambia esto si sí recibiste uno].
>
> Thank you,
> [tu nombre]

> Hello, thanks for your reply. Unfortunately the problem is **not** resolved: Actions is still blocked on **sanmiguelyt955** as of [fecha]. I just tested again: [describe: "a new workflow run did not start" / "the Actions tab shows X"].
> Could you please (1) tell me the exact cause, (2) escalate this to the team that can lift the restriction, and (3) keep the ticket open until I confirm it works? Thank you.

Please review the history of those tickets, escalate to the Actions/Trust & Safety team, and tell me the exact reason and steps to restore it. I am available to test right away. Thank you.

## 4. Corto (para redes: @GitHubSupport en X)
> @GitHubSupport Hi! GitHub Actions has been disabled on my account (sanmiguelyt955) for [tiempo]. My tickets #[A] #[B] got replies but were closed without a fix. Could someone please escalate? Thanks!

## Consejos
- Pide siempre **la causa exacta** y **que no cierren el ticket** hasta que confirmes.
- Responde **en el mismo hilo** para mantener el historial, y cita los números anteriores en los nuevos.
- Adjunta una captura de la pestaña Actions o del aviso.
- Revisa **Settings → Billing and plans**: un método de pago pendiente o un límite de gasto en 0 USD también bloquea Actions.
