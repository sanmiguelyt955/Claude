You always pick the slowest line at the supermarket. And no, it's not bad luck. Kubo picks line number two. Short line, fast cashier, perfect. Then the lady in front of him pulls out a coupon from a different store. Line one moves. Line three moves. Kubo's line doesn't move at all. The guy who was behind him in line one is already leaving with a birthday cake. Here's the math. With three lines, only one of them can be the fastest. So the chance that yours wins is just one in three. Which means two out of three times, another line beats you. And your brain makes it even worse. When your line wins, you don't even notice. When it loses, you stare at it the whole time. So Kubo did the smart thing and switched lines. And that's exactly when his old line started flying. Switching doesn't save you. It just gives you twice as many chances to lose. Send this to the friend who always switches lines. Now your turn. With four lines, what are the chances that yours is the fastest?
